Privacy Policy

Renuqo keeps track of when your documents expire. There is no account, and the documents you add never leave your iPhone. To learn which of our ads lead to installs and purchases, the app uses two services; what they see is listed below.

What stays on your iPhone

The documents you add (names, categories, numbers, issuers, dates and notes) and the photos, PDFs and scans you attach are stored in Renuqo’s own storage on your iPhone and are never uploaded. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, this data is part of that backup, which Apple’s privacy policy covers. We never have access to it.

App lock

If you turn on Lock Renuqo in Settings, the app asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode each time it opens. iOS does the check; Renuqo only learns whether it succeeded and never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document, and the scan is saved on your iPhone. When you attach a photo, Renuqo receives only the photo you pick, not your library.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service and no server is involved.

What we collect

Two services help us see which of our ads work. They receive technical and purchase information only, never your documents, notes or attachments. RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) records your Renuqo Pro purchase made through Apple under a random app user ID, and passes on Apple’s ad attribution token once for each installation. AppsFlyer (appsflyer.com) counts installs and app openings under a random identifier, together with the device model, iOS version, language, an approximate region derived from the IP address, and the Renuqo Pro purchase that RevenueCat forwards to it. This is used to measure advertising and purchases and for nothing else. Each company’s own privacy policy applies to how it handles the data.

Tracking

Renuqo does not track you. It does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not show Apple’s tracking permission prompt and does not combine anything about you with data from other companies’ apps or websites.

Purchases

Renuqo Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and we never see your payment details. Whether you have Pro is decided on your iPhone through Apple’s StoreKit; RevenueCat only observes the purchase for the statistics above. A yearly subscription can start with a free trial for new subscribers; the App Store shows the terms before you confirm.

Children

Renuqo is not aimed at children and asks nobody for personal information. The statistics above are collected the same way for every user.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app to remove it together with its attachment. Deleting Renuqo removes everything it stored on your iPhone. To have the install and purchase records removed from the two services, write to the address under Contact below; we will ask them to delete whatever can be matched to your installation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the foot of this page changes with it.
