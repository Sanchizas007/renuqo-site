Privacy Policy

Renuqo keeps track of when your documents expire. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics: nothing you enter is collected, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

The documents you add (names, categories, numbers, issuers, dates and notes) and the photos, PDFs and scans you attach are stored in Renuqo’s own storage on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, this data is part of that backup, which Apple’s privacy policy covers. We never have access to it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document, and the scan is saved on your iPhone. When you attach a photo, Renuqo receives only the photo you pick, not your library.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service and no server is involved.

What is not collected

No identifiers, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no advertising and no third-party code of any kind.

Purchases

Renuqo Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. The app checks on your iPhone, through Apple’s StoreKit, whether you have Pro.

Children

Renuqo collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app to remove it together with its attachment. Deleting Renuqo removes everything it stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the foot of this page changes with it.
